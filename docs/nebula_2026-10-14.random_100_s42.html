Method and caveats

Sentiment and complaints: fine-tuned RoBERTa (en) / XLM-R (other languages), int8 ONNX. Negative if P(negative) ≥ 0.30 (missing a negative review costs more than a false alarm). A separate head flags complaints, also inside positive reviews.

Uncertainty: shares use Wilson 95% intervals; the mean rating a t-interval with the finite-population correction (a random sample without replacement from a known pool).

Small collection (n=100): phrase and driver statistics need a few hundred reviews; use the pool collection for those.

No themes for this app: the theme taxonomy (data/themes.json) is built per app and covers Notion. Problem areas come from the phrases that set negative reviews apart; build a taxonomy to get named themes (README).
