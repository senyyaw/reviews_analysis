Phrases that set negative reviews apart

Fightin' Words z-scores: right = typical of negative reviews, left = typical of the rest. Generic sentiment words are removed, so what remains are product aspects.

Rating drivers

Odds of a 1–2★ (vs 4–5★) rating when a review mentions the aspect: logistic regression, cross-validated AUC 0.93, only aspects whose sign holds in ≥ 90% of 200 bootstraps. Associations, not causes.

Method and caveats

Sentiment and complaints: fine-tuned RoBERTa (en) / XLM-R (other languages), int8 ONNX. Negative if P(negative) ≥ 0.30 (missing a negative review costs more than a false alarm). A separate head flags complaints, also inside positive reviews.

Phrases: Fightin' Words (Monroe et al. 2008), informative Dirichlet prior from 14 other productivity apps + this corpus; document-level counts; generic sentiment and filler words removed.

Drivers: L2 logistic regression, 1-2 vs 4-5 stars, aspect n-grams; C by 5-fold CV; 200 bootstraps, sign stability >= 0.9.

Uncertainty: shares use Wilson 95% intervals; the mean rating a t-interval.

No themes for this app: the theme taxonomy (data/themes.json) is built per app and covers Notion. Problem areas come from the phrases that set negative reviews apart; build a taxonomy to get named themes (README).
